Non-Discrimination Statement

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Standard starting-point language. If any program — the Food Pantry, for one — ever receives federal or state funding, that funder may require exact statutory wording (USDA-funded food programs, for example, have a specific required paragraph). Check with each funding source before treating this as final.

Paint 4 Peace Children's Village is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive environment for every child, family, volunteer, staff member, and partner we work with.

Our commitment

Paint 4 Peace does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, marital or family status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local law. This applies to:

  • Enrollment and participation in our programs
  • Hiring, employment, and volunteer opportunities
  • Access to our facilities, services, and resources

Equal opportunity

We are an equal opportunity organization. All applicants for employment or volunteer positions are considered on their qualifications, without regard to any protected characteristic.

Reasonable accommodations

We want our programs to be genuinely accessible. If you or your child need a reasonable accommodation to take part in our programs, let us know and we'll work with you.

Reporting a concern

If you believe you've experienced discrimination in connection with Paint 4 Peace's programs or services, please contact us directly:
